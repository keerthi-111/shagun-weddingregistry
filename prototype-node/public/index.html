How it works

For the couple

Create your registry

Your names, date, city, a note for guests, and your UPI ID.

Add gifts from anywhere

Paste a product link — we pull in the name, photo and price. Add cash funds with a goal.

Share one link

On WhatsApp, your wedding website, or print the QR on your card.

For guests

Open the link

No sign-up, no app download.

Buy or contribute

Buy on the original store, or pay any amount via GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app.

Mark it & bless

Mark it gifted so nobody duplicates, and leave a message for the couple.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Creating and sharing a registry costs nothing, and there's no cut taken from cash gifts.

Where does the money from cash funds go?

Directly to your UPI ID. Guests pay from their own UPI app — Shagun never holds or touches the money.

How do physical gifts reach us?

Guests buy on the store's own site and ship to the address you share with them, or bring it to the wedding.

Will guests buy the same thing twice?

Once a guest marks a gift as bought, it shows as gifted to everyone else.

Can I edit my registry after sharing it?

Anytime. The link stays the same.
